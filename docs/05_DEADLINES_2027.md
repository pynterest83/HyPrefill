## Caveats
- "Estimated" rows are extrapolated by adding one year to the most recent confirmed edition's dates; treat day-level precision as approximate.
- USENIX ATC was discontinued by USENIX after the 2025 edition; ACM SIGOPS revived it (still branded "ATC"). A 2027 edition is expected but has no published CFP as of 2026-09-14.
- ASPLOS runs 2 submission cycles/year (not 3, despite older conventions) as of the 2026/2027 editions.
- ICSE dropped its 2-cycle format starting with the 2027 edition — there is now a single research-track deadline.
- Several official pages blocked direct fetch (HTTP 403); those rows were cross-verified via cached/secondary sources as noted in the research and should be spot-checked against the live site before relying on them for a real submission.
